# 🚗 Automobile Insurance Complaints Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

**Project ID:** CDACL-001  
**Domain:** Automobile Insurance  
**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI  
**Dataset Period:** 2010–2016  

This project analyzes automobile insurance complaints filed against insurance companies operating in New York State.

The objective is to identify complaint patterns, analyze premium trends, compare insurance companies, and generate meaningful business insights using Power BI.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze different types of insurance complaints.
- Identify yearly complaint trends.
- Examine changes in premiums over multiple years.
- Compare five insurance companies.
- Identify companies that may require improvement.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### Complaint Analysis

- Not Upheld complaints account for 57.7% of total complaints.
- Question of Fact complaints account for 30.7%.
- Upheld complaints account for 11.6%.
- The highest annual complaint total occurred in 2011.

### Premium Analysis

- Aggregate premiums increased between 2011 and 2015.
- The highest annual aggregate premium value was recorded in 2015.
- The 2015 and 2016 aggregate premium values are identical.

### Company Comparison

Five companies were selected based on their total complaint volumes:

1. GEICO General Insurance Company
2. Allstate Insurance Company
3. Country-Wide Insurance Company
4. State Farm Mutual Automobile Insurance Company
5. GEICO Indemnity Company

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Improve complaint resolution processes.
2. Investigate recurring upheld complaints.
3. Monitor companies with higher complaint ratios.
4. Improve customer communication and claims handling.
5. Investigate premium fluctuations using additional market and claims data.

## ⚠️ Data Limitations

- The dataset contains only two company records for 2010.
- Annual record counts vary between years.
- Claims frequency and claim severity data are unavailable.
- Economic and regulatory factors are not included.
- Complaint counts alone cannot determine overall company performance.
- Premium trends cannot establish the causes of premium fluctuations.
